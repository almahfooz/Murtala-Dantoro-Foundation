Our programmes

Five areas guide our work with residents, local leaders and partner organisations, from the classroom to the community health centre.

On this pageEducationHealthLivelihoodsCommunityAdvocacy

Education support

Helping students access learning materials, prepare for exams and study in better-equipped classrooms.

  • Scholarships and learning materials for students from low-income households
  • Free tutorials and holiday lessons for WAEC, NECO and UTME examinations
  • School rehabilitation, literacy activities and digital learning outreach

Community healthcare

Bringing preventive care, health information and basic services closer to underserved communities.

  • Community health outreach and screenings for conditions such as hypertension and diabetes
  • Maternal and child health support
  • Health education on hygiene, nutrition and disease prevention, alongside the distribution of basic health kits

Skills and livelihoods

Supporting young people and women as they build practical skills, develop business ideas and earn an income.

  • Vocational and technical training
  • Entrepreneurship mentoring and financial literacy workshops
  • Small-business grants and start-up support

Community development

Working with residents on clean water, shared facilities and practical support for families.

  • Boreholes and local water supply projects
  • Renovation of schools, health centres and public spaces
  • Sanitation campaigns and support for local cooperatives, farmers and artisans

Advocacy and civic participation

Creating opportunities for residents, especially women and young people, to take part in decisions that affect them.

  • Town hall meetings, community forums and conversations with local leaders
  • Awareness activities on equality, inclusion and human rights
  • Voter education and opportunities for young people to take part in civic life
