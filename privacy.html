Privacy notice

How information moves when you use this website or contact the foundation.

Sending an enquiry

The contact form asks for your name, email address, a topic and your message. When you select Send enquiry, the form sends these details through Web3Forms for delivery to the foundation’s inbox. The team uses them to respond. You can also contact the foundation directly by phone or email.

Donation information

The donation page lists bank transfer details. You arrange a transfer with your bank. If you request an acknowledgement, share only the information needed to identify the contribution. Keep card numbers, passwords and online banking codes private.

Videos and social channels

Video and social links open third-party services, including YouTube, Facebook, TikTok and WhatsApp. Visiting these services is subject to their own privacy policies.

Privacy enquiries

For questions about information you have shared, or a request about a photograph in the gallery, contact the foundation at info@murtaladantorofoundation.org.ng.
