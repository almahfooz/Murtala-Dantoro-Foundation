Murtala Dantoro Foundation
Website files

Open the site on your computer
On Windows, right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All. On a Mac, double-click the ZIP to extract it. Open the extracted folder and double-click index.html. Keep the assets folder beside the HTML files.

What is included
The folder contains the homepage, About us, Our work, Project appeals, Field gallery, Get involved, Donate, Contact and Privacy notice. The gallery includes 18 original field photographs. The foundation logo, photographs and fonts are included locally. The former programme, events, blog and video page addresses forward to their new destinations.

Editing and hosting
Edit the HTML files in a text editor. The shared styles and interactive features are in assets/site.css and assets/site.js. Upload the HTML files and the complete assets folder to your web host, keeping the same folder structure. No installation or build step is required. If you host on a different domain, update the og:url and og:image metadata in the page heads for shared links.

Connected services
The pages, photos, photo viewer and navigation work from the extracted folder. Sending an enquiry, watching videos, following social links and sending email use external services. The contact page keeps the Web3Forms routing from the original site and offers a direct email alternative.

Before a public launch
Confirm the two Providus Bank accounts, beneficiary name, office details, management team and contact inbox with the foundation. Test one authorised enquiry from the final domain and confirm that it arrives. For international transfers, obtain the current SWIFT and routing instructions from the foundation. The accounts are reproduced from the original donation page. Old template counters have been omitted.

Assets
The original black and white MDF logos are preserved in assets/images. Full field photographs retain their original captions. The site uses local web fonts with their notices in assets/fonts/LICENSES.txt.
